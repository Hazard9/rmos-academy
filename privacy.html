Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

What we collect

When you sign up for RMOS Academy, we collect your email address, and your first name if you choose to provide it. We don't collect anything beyond what's needed to send you the course.

How it's used

Your email address is used to deliver the seven RMOS Academy lessons, the three free manager tools, and occasional updates about RMOS. It is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their own marketing.

Email delivery

Academy emails are sent through Kit, our email service provider. Kit processes your email address on our behalf to deliver these messages and is bound by its own privacy and data practices.

Unsubscribing

Every email includes an unsubscribe link. You can opt out at any time, and doing so removes you from future sends immediately.

Analytics and cookies

This site does not currently run third-party analytics or tracking cookies beyond what Kit itself may use to operate the signup form. If that changes, this policy will be updated to reflect it.

Requesting deletion

To request that your information be deleted, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] with the email address you signed up with. We'll remove your information from our systems and confirm once it's done.
