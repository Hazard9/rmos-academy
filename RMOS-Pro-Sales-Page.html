Questions

Before you buy

Do I need the Manual before the Bootcamp?

Not strictly, the Bootcamp explains what it needs as it goes. But the Manual gives you the full reasoning behind each move, and most people find the six weeks click faster once they've read it. The Bundle includes both either way.

What's actually different between the Manual and the Bootcamp?

The Manual is sixteen chapters, read once, kept as a reference. The Bootcamp is six worksheets, one a week, built for you to write in using your real floor and your real team. One teaches the system, the other installs it.

Will this work for my specific type of store?

The five pillars and the Health Score are deliberately generic. They're written to sit on top of whatever terminology and standards your store already uses, not replace them.

Do I need RMOS Pro, or does the paper system work on its own?

The Manual, Bootcamp, and Toolkit are a complete paper system on their own. RMOS Pro is the same system running as software, for when you're ready to stop tracking it by hand. Neither one requires the other.

How long before I see a difference in my Health Score?

The Bootcamp is built around a six week install, and most of that is habit forming, not score chasing. The score becomes a reliable trend line somewhere around week three or four, once you've got a few weeks of honest scoring behind you.
